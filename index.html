Problem

Action

Improved the language used in calls and follow-up, strengthened appointment confirmation, and focused on giving customers a clear reason to come in.

Result

The process supported a 75–83% appointment show rate across tracked performance periods.

Building Better Lead Follow-Up

Problem

Internet leads often lose interest when follow-up is slow, generic, or inconsistent.

Action

Used CRM workflows, stronger call scripts, and better timing to keep the conversation moving.

Result

Follow-up became more consistent, appointment volume improved, and customers had a clearer path from online interest to dealership visit.

Coaching a Remote BDC Team

Problem

Remote BDC work can become inconsistent without clear expectations and daily accountability.

Action

Coached team members on lead timing, CRM habits, objection handling, and communication tone.

Result

The team stayed focused on daily activity, appointment quality, and measurable sales outcomes.

Objection Handling Philosophy

Good objection handling starts with listening. When a customer says they are just looking, worried about price, or not ready to come in, there is usually a deeper concern underneath. I try to identify that concern, answer it clearly, and give the customer a next step that feels reasonable.
